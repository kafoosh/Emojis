The Diamond Trick

A self-working illusion powered by the PATEO principle. Play fair… the 💎 still wins. Every. Single. Time. 😏

how is this even possible??

PATEO = Point At Two, Eliminate One. You and the Magician take turns. The pointer chooses two emojis; the other person destroys one of them. Keep going until a single emoji survives.

  • When you point, the Magician quietly eliminates — never the diamond. 💎
  • When the Magician points, it only ever offers you two non-diamonds, so whichever you smash, the diamond is safe.

With the turn order set just right, the math guarantees the 💎 is the last one standing. No sleight of hand — pure parity. 🧠
